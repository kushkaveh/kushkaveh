## Hello.

I'm **Kush** — a multidisciplinary builder working across **AI, product, design, engineering, and business**.

I started in visual design and branding, moved deeper into UX and product, then into software and AI. I never really left any of those disciplines behind.

They compounded.

Today I build intelligent products, organizational systems, digital experiences, and new ways of working — usually taking something from a messy business problem all the way to a working product.

Currently **Head of AI at ePlanet**.

But titles aren't really the interesting part.

**I like hard problems, beautiful systems, and actually shipping things.**

## Things I Care About

**AI that solves real problems** — not AI added because someone needed an AI feature.

**Taste in the age of cheap execution** — when everyone can generate, judgment becomes more valuable.

**Executable design** — the boundary between design and implementation is getting increasingly blurry.

**Multidisciplinary builders** — AI is changing how much one capable person can understand and create.

**Human judgment** — automation should increase capability, not eliminate thinking.

**Systems over symptoms** — fixing the interface doesn't help if the underlying workflow is broken.
